M4F — experimental rigid-body pose trace

Development only · ideal bilateral guide experiment. Primitive bodies and datum lines are diagnostics. These are recorded dynamics states, with no live simulation, wheel-contact model, production train assets or editor integration.

CarBogieAxle carrier

Two cars, three bogies with one shared middle bogie, six axle carriers. Lines connect actual bearing anchors. Short normal segments are solid for running datums and dashed for side datums. Boxes use actual body orientation and provisional dimensions. World coordinates: X forward, Y up, Z lateral; metres. The camera fits the recorded assembly; source datum lines can leave the view.

25 states at 1/240 s span only 0.100 s of physical time. Replay is slowed 24×, with discrete frames. No interpolation, force smoothing or track-following animation is applied. Space: replay/pause. Left/right: state. Escape: pause.

Physical alignment diagnostics
